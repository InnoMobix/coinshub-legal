Coins Hub Account Deletion

This page explains how users can request deletion of their Coins Hub account and associated personal data.

How to delete your account

You can delete your account directly inside the Coins Hub application:

  1. Open the Coins Hub app.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Go to Profile.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm account deletion.
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What data will be deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be deleted:

  • Your Coins Hub user account
  • Your personal coin collection
  • Your saved coins, wishlist, or favorites
  • Your app preferences connected to your account

What data may be retained

No personal data is retained after account deletion.

Data deletion period

Account data and all associated information are permanently deleted immediately after the deletion request is confirmed.
